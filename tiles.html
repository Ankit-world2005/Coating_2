Delhi NCR groundwater (Gurgaon, Noida, and South Delhi) is exceptionally high in dissolved calcium carbonate and magnesium salts (TDS > 800 ppm). As droplets dry on untreated glass, these minerals chemically bond into microscopic silica pores, causing permanent cloudy etching that no conventional soap can remove.

CoatingWale Bathroom Shield covalently bonds fluoro-silane oligomers directly into the glass and ceramic silica lattice. This fills all microscopic peaks and valleys, lowering surface energy from 75 dynes/cm to less than 18 dynes/cm. Mineral-laden water cannot flatten or cling; droplets bead up and slide off into the drain before evaporation occurs.

Engineered for all wet area surfaces in residential and hotel washrooms:

  • Shower Glass Partitions (Framed & Frameless): Total immunity from hard water white lines and soap scum.
  • Glazed Ceramic Wall Tiles: Prevents dirty water retention on tile faces.
  • Vitreous China Washbasins: Keeps toothpaste residue and makeup oils sliding away effortlessly.
  • Mirrors & Vanity Glazing: Anti-steam and zero mineral misting.

Executed in under 2 hours per washroom:

  • Stage 1 - Micro-Abrasive Glass Polishing: Specialized rotary machine polishing with cerium oxide to eliminate existing limescale.
  • Stage 2 - Pure Solvent Degrease: Removes residual soap oils, shampoo wax, and limescale residues.
  • Stage 3 - Cross-Directional Nano Application: Molecular bonding layer hand-buffed to optical brilliance.
  • Stage 4 - Hydrophobic Testing: Live water spray demonstration showing immediate 115° contact angle beading.

Cement grout is porous and acts as a sponge for dampness, creating black mold spots within months. Our deep-penetrating siloxane grout sealer locks out moisture permanently, keeping grout bright white or charcoal without discoloration.

Once coated, harsh hydrochloric toilet acids and abrasive scourers are completely banned and unnecessary:

  • Rinse glass with clean water after showers and swipe with a soft squeegee.
  • Wipe tile walls once a week with a microfiber towel and diluted neutral dish soap.
  • Never use harsh acid cleaners: they dissolve metallic hinges and silicone perimeter gaskets.
