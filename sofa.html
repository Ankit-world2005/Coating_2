COMPLETE ENGINEERING BREAKDOWN & GUIDELINES

Expand the dropdowns below for fiber encapsulation science, textile suitability, spill blotting protocols, and warranty guidelines.

Conventional supermarket stain sprays create a stiff, sticky surface layer that seals air spaces, alters the soft feel of delicate fabrics, and breaks down under light friction.

CoatingWale Fabric Armor uses solvent-free colloidal silicon dioxide nano-capsules. When electrostatically atomized, the formulation wraps around each individual textile fiber without filling the gaps between woven threads. The fabric continues to breathe freely while the surface energy is lowered to a level where water, milk, and wine cannot penetrate the weave.

Tested and safe for both synthetic and natural luxury textiles:

  • Pure Linen & Cotton Blends: Prevents rapid capillary absorption of dark liquids.
  • Velvet & Chenille Upholstery: Eliminates water spots and fiber crushing.
  • Polyester, Microfibers & Suede: Exceptional adhesion with rapid drying.
  • Dining Room Chairs & Headboards: Shielded against food oils and greasy fingerprints.

Completed inside your home in under 90 minutes without odor:

  • Stage 1 - High-Power Dry Vacuuming: Deep HEPA extraction of embedded dust, pet dander, and loose lint.
  • Stage 2 - Discreet Patch Test: 2-minute compatibility spot check on a hidden seam.
  • Stage 3 - Electrostatic Atomization: Ultra-fine uniform micro-mist ensuring 360° fiber penetration.
  • Stage 4 - Ambient Curing: Cures tack-free within 4 hours; full stain immunity ready by evening.

When a spill occurs on coated upholstery:

  • Gently place a dry paper towel or clean microfiber cloth on the bead.
  • The liquid will absorb into the towel automatically via capillary action.
  • Never rub aggressively: Rubbing forces liquid mechanically through the weave mesh.
