COMPLETE ENGINEERING BREAKDOWN & GUIDELINES

Expand the dropdowns below for scientific formulation details, substrate compatibility, application protocols, and warranty metrics.

Conventional tinted films absorb heat and trap it within the glass pane, frequently causing glass thermal breakage or unsightly peeling edges. CoatingWale Glass Armor utilizes a proprietary sol-gel liquid formulation enriched with antimony tin oxide (ATO) and cesium tungsten bronze nano-particles.

These nano-particles are molecularly sized to selectively reflect infrared wavelengths (780nm–2500nm) back outwards while permitting high visible light transmission (380nm–740nm). The cured coating creates a permanent inorganic bond with the silica matrix of the glass, becoming an integrated physical extension of the surface that cannot bubble, crack, or peel.

Our formulation is engineered for 100% compatibility across architectural residential and commercial glazing:

  • Monolithic Clear Float Glass (4mm–12mm): Full thermal rejection upgrade without altering structural load.
  • Toughened & Tempered Safety Glazing: Perfect for high-rise balconies, full-height villa windows, and sliding doors.
  • Double Glazed Units (DGU / IGU): Applied internally to enhance low-E thermal insulation without voiding manufacturer seals.
  • Skylights & Atriums: Eliminates the greenhouse effect in double-height courtyards and roof lights.
  • Laminated Security Glass: Applied directly to the outer surface with zero polymer interlayer delamination risk.

Every application is executed by certified in-house CoatingWale technicians following strict cleanroom-standard SOPs:

  • Stage 1 - Decontamination: Multi-pass glass scrub with cerium oxide slurry to remove hard water scale, silicone oils, and atmospheric fallout.
  • Stage 2 - Isopropyl Degrease: Pure IPA flash wipe to reach zero surface hydrocarbon residue.
  • Stage 3 - Perimeter Masking: High-precision framing protection preventing overspray on anodized aluminum or UPVC mullions.
  • Stage 4 - Controlled Flow Application: Proprietary laminar flow application ensuring a microscopic 7–9 micron uniform protective thickness.
  • Stage 5 - UV & Ambient Cross-linking: Accelerated infrared curing to achieve full mechanical pencil hardness (6H+).
  • Stage 6 - Spectrophotometer Handover: On-site transmission testing comparing uncoated vs. coated glass before client sign-off.

In accordance with CoatingWale transparency principles, our coating cannot solve:

  • Pre-existing cracks, glass fractures, or structural frame distortions.
  • Fogging or moisture condensation located *inside* failed double-glazed sealed air gaps.
  • Severe deep glass gouges caused by metal tools (requires glass replacement).

Our site assessment technicians verify glass integrity prior to commencing work.

Every residential and commercial project receives an official tamper-proof CoatingWale Warranty Certificate covering:

  • Zero peeling, flaking, or bubbling from the glass substrate.
  • Zero yellowing, hazing, or loss of optical clarity under intense UV.
  • Maintenance of at least 80% original infrared solar rejection efficiency for a full decade.
  • Free re-inspection and remediation support should any adhesion failure occur.

Cured nano-ceramic glass is exceptionally easy to maintain. We recommend:

  • Wipe with clean water and standard microfiber cloth or rubber squeegee.
  • Use mild pH-neutral glass cleaners if needed.
  • Avoid abrasive scouring pads, steel wool, or concentrated acid washes.
