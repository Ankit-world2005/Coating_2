Concrete roof slabs absorb up to 90% of solar radiation during peak Delhi NCR summer days, heating up to 58°C and radiating thermal energy into top-floor bedrooms and offices throughout the night.

CoatingWale Cool-Roof incorporates hollow ceramic micro-spheres and pure rutile titanium dioxide suspended in a cross-linked elastomeric polyurethane-acrylic polymer emulsion. It reflects 88% of incoming solar rays back into the atmosphere and emits 91% of absorbed heat rapidly, preventing conductive thermal soaking through concrete.

Engineered for high adhesion across all common Indian roofing decks:

  • Cast-in-Situ Reinforced Concrete (RCC): Deep penetration nano-primer seals micro-capillaries.
  • China Mosaic / Broken Tile Surfaces: Excellent bonding over glazed ceramic chips without chipping.
  • Pre-Existing Cementitious Screeds & IPS: Bridges hairline thermal expansion cracks.
  • Industrial Pre-Engineered Metal Roof Sheets: Eliminates expansion noise and drops factory ambient temperatures.
  • Bituminous & APP Membrane Roofs: Protects aging asphalt from UV degradation with specialized primer.

Executed by certified CoatingWale roof restoration crews:

  • Stage 1 - High-Pressure Washing (250 Bar): Strips dirt, moss, loose cement, and previous flaky paint layers.
  • Stage 2 - Crack Repair & Parapet V-Grooving: Polyurethane sealant injected into structural fissures up to 3mm.
  • Stage 3 - Silane-Siloxane Hydrophobic Primer: Sub-surface moisture block penetrating 5mm into concrete pores.
  • Stage 4 - Intermediate Cool Coat with Fiber Mesh: Reinforces expansion joints and drains against thermal movement.
  • Stage 5 - High-Albedo Topcoat: Dual cross-directional application to achieve an impenetrable 800+ micron dry film thickness (DFT).

CoatingWale integrity guidelines:

  • Severe continuous water logging caused by negative drainage slope must be corrected with civil leveling prior to topcoat.
  • Active pipe burst leaks inside plumbing shafts require mechanical plumbing repairs.

Covering thermal solar reflectance stability, membrane adhesion, and resistance to monsoon UV embrittlement. Certified thermal imaging inspection report provided upon project completion.
